# CaniClip — Logo Guidelines

## 1. The logo
- **Idea:** a selection frame whose top-right corner turns into a question mark. It is the name, *Can I clip?*, in one shape: the crop frame is the clip and the hook is the question.
- **Versions:** horizontal lockup (primary) · stacked lockup · symbol · wordmark.
- **Files:** `masters/` holds the source SVGs. `symbol/`, `lockups/`, `web/` and `macos/` hold ready-to-use exports. All artwork is outlined: no live text, no strokes.

## 2. Construction
- One stroke weight, one corner radius and one dot. Symbol: 26-unit stroke, 28-unit corner radius, 16-unit dot radius on a 256 grid.
- The wordmark is custom, built from the same parts: monoline lowercase, round ends, circular bowls, and i-dots drawn like the question's dot. Do not retype it in a font.

## 3. Clear space
Keep a clear zone of **one dot diameter** around the logo on every side. The dot is the round point under the question mark; the zone scales with the logo.

## 4. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal lockup | 96 px wide | 25 mm wide |
| Stacked lockup | 64 px wide | 18 mm wide |
| Symbol | 16 px — use the **small cut** (`*-small-*`, `*-favicon*`) at 32 px and below | 6 mm |

## 5. Colour
| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK (approx.) | Pantone (nearest) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clip Yellow | #FFC83D | 255 200 61 | 0 22 76 0 | 1225 C |
| Ink | #16161A | 22 22 26 | 70 60 55 90 | Black 6 C |

CMYK and Pantone values are approximations; confirm with your printer against a physical swatch.

**Approved pairs**
| Logo | Background | Contrast |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | White or light neutrals | 18.1 : 1 |
| Ink | Clip Yellow (app icon, tiles, merch) | 11.7 : 1 |
| Clip Yellow (reversed artwork) | Ink or other near-black | 11.7 : 1 |
| White (reversed artwork) | Ink, dark photos, dark UI | 18.1 : 1 |

Never set Clip Yellow on white (1.6 : 1, it disappears). Use the `-reversed` / `-white` files on dark backgrounds: they are drawn about 6 % thinner so they do not look heavier than the ink version.

## 6. App and menu bar
- **macOS app icon:** Ink symbol on a Clip Yellow tile (`macos/AppIcon.appiconset`). The 16, 32 and 64 px slots use the small cut.
- **Menu bar:** `macos/MenuBarIcon.imageset`, small cut, black on transparent, marked as a template image so macOS tints it for light, dark and active states. Size 18 pt.
- **Web:** `web/` contains `favicon.ico`, `favicon.svg`, Apple touch icon, PWA icons, `site.webmanifest` and a `<head>` snippet.

## 7. Typography
- **The wordmark is artwork**, not type.
- **In the app:** SF Pro (the macOS system font), as the UI already uses it.
- **Marketing and web:** a rounded or geometric sans with an open licence pairs well, for example Inter (SIL Open Font License). SF Pro is licensed for Apple-platform interfaces only, not for logos or general marketing.

## 8. Don'ts
Don't stretch, squash or rotate · don't recolour outside Ink, Clip Yellow and white · don't put Clip Yellow on white · don't add shadows, outlines or gradients to the mark (the subtle tile gradient on the app icon is part of the icon, not the logo) · don't change the symbol–wordmark spacing or size ratio · don't use the regular symbol below 32 px · don't retype the wordmark.

## 9. Source
All masters are generated by `design/logo/build_logo.py`; change geometry there and re-run rather than editing SVGs by hand.
