What we heard

The brief

A native macOS menu bar app for screenshots, annotation and GIF recording. The name asks a question, "Can I clip?", so the mark should carry both the question and the act of capturing, and work from a 16 px menu bar icon to a Dock tile.

quickcuriousprecisefriendlynative

Success: avoids camera and aperture clichés, readable at 16 px, works in one colour, distinct in the Dock.

Final design

Question Frame

A selection frame whose top-right corner turns into a question mark: Can I clip?

  • Holds the whole name in one shape: the crop frame is the clip, the hook is the question
  • Built from one stroke, one corner radius and one dot, so the wordmark grows from the same parts
  • A small-size cut keeps the counter and dot gap open in the 16 px menu bar
  • Clip Yellow is rare in the Dock and reads as the annotation highlighter
